Best Phones for Students in Malaysia (2026)

Published September 2026 · By SpecShelf Editorial · 5 min read

As a student, your phone needs to last all day, handle classes and assignments, and not cost a fortune. Here are the best picks for Malaysian students in 2026.

What Students Need in a Phone

Before we get to the picks, here's what matters most for a student phone:

  • Battery life — You need to get through a full day of classes without charging.
  • Performance — Smooth enough for notes, research, video calls, and the occasional game.
  • Storage — At least 128GB for apps, photos, and documents.
  • Price — Under RM 1,500 is the sweet spot for most students.
  • Camera — Good enough for scanning notes and the occasional photo.

Our Top Picks

1. Redmi Note 14 Pro 5G — Best Overall Value

From RM 1,199

The Redmi Note 14 Pro 5G is hard to beat at this price. You get a 6.67-inch AMOLED display, a 200MP main camera, 5G connectivity, and a large 5500mAh battery with 45W fast charging. The Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 chip handles everyday tasks and light gaming with ease. For students who want the most phone for their money, this is the one.

Our verdict: Best all-rounder for students. Camera, battery, 5G, and performance — all covered at a price that won't stress your budget.

2. Samsung Galaxy A35 5G — Best for Reliability

From RM 1,299

Samsung's A-series has always been known for reliability and long software support. The Galaxy A35 5G gets four years of OS updates and five years of security patches — meaning this phone will stay secure and updated well past your graduation. The 5000mAh battery easily lasts a full day, and the 50MP camera is great for everyday use.

Our verdict: Best for students who want a phone that lasts the whole degree. Long update support means you won't need to replace it anytime soon.

3. Realme 13 Pro — Best Camera for the Price

From RM 1,099

If you care about photography — whether for social media, projects, or just memories — the Realme 13 Pro punches well above its price. It features a Sony IMX890 sensor with optical image stabilisation, which means sharper photos even in low light. The curved AMOLED display also makes it look much more premium than its price suggests.

Our verdict: Best camera under RM 1,200 for students. Great for content creators, social media, and anyone who wants photos that actually look good.

4. POCO X6 Pro — Best for Gaming Students

From RM 1,199

If you game on your phone between classes, the POCO X6 Pro is the pick. It runs on the Dimensity 8300 Ultra chip — one of the fastest mid-range processors available — with a 144Hz display for buttery smooth gameplay. Despite the gaming focus, battery life is solid at 5000mAh with 67W charging.

Our verdict: Best for students who game. The Dimensity 8300 Ultra is genuinely fast — no throttling, no heating issues, smooth from morning to night.

5. iPhone 15 — Best if Budget Allows

From RM 3,399

If your parents are generous or you have savings, the iPhone 15 is worth considering. It holds its resale value better than any Android phone, gets iOS updates for at least 5–6 years, and the ecosystem (iCloud, AirDrop, iMessage) is hard to leave once you're in. The camera system is also excellent for video content creators. It's expensive, but it's a long-term investment.

Our verdict: Best long-term investment if budget isn't a concern. Resale value, software support, and ecosystem lock-in make it a smart buy — just expensive upfront.
