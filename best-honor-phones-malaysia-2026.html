Best Honor Phones in Malaysia 2026 — Full Lineup Ranked

Honor sells everything in Malaysia from tough budget X-series phones to the Magic flagship and foldable range. The lineup changed a lot this year: the Honor 600 series launched here first in April, the Magic V6 foldable followed in June, and the X9e Pro with its 11,000 mAh battery arrived in September. This guide ranks the Honor phones that make sense to buy right now, using launch prices announced for Malaysia, and tells you plainly where an older model no longer makes sense.

1. Honor Magic8 Pro — Best Honor Flagship in Malaysia

RM 4,599 (12GB + 512GB) · RM 5,199 (16GB + 1TB) · Malaysia launch prices

The Honor Magic8 Pro is Honor's top regular flagship in Malaysia. Malaysia was the first market outside China to get it, and it runs the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset with MagicOS 10 on Android 16. Honor's focus is the camera: a 50MP main sensor, a 200MP periscope telephoto with 3.7x optical zoom, a 50MP ultrawide and a 50MP selfie camera.

The 7,100 mAh battery is large for a flagship, and charging goes up to 100W wired and 80W wireless. The screen is a 6.71-inch LTPO OLED with 6,000 nits peak brightness, which helps outdoors in Malaysian sunlight. Retailers such as Senheng and Harvey Norman have listed the 16GB + 1TB model slightly below Honor's own RM 5,199 price, so it is worth comparing before you buy.

2. Honor 600 Pro — Flagship Chip Under RM 3,500

RM 3,099 (12GB + 256GB) · RM 3,299 (12GB + 512GB)

The Honor 600 Pro launched in Malaysia in April 2026 as the first market worldwide. It is the first phone in Honor's N series to use a top-tier Snapdragon 8 Elite chip, which is why it is positioned as an "accessible flagship". It also has a 200MP camera and a 7,000 mAh battery with 80W wired and 50W wireless charging.

The 6.57-inch AMOLED display reaches 6,500 nits peak brightness and the phone carries IP68/IP69K water resistance. If the Magic8 Pro is more than you want to spend, this is the closest Honor to flagship performance for around RM 3,000.

3. Honor Magic V6 — Best Foldable Honor

RM 7,699 (16GB + 512GB) · RM 8,899 (16GB + 1TB)

The Honor Magic V6 is a book-style foldable that launched in Malaysia in June 2026. It is very slim for a foldable at about 4mm when unfolded, and Honor says its 6,660 mAh silicon-carbon battery is the largest ever fitted in a foldable. It was also the first foldable to use the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5.

Honor rates the Super Steel hinge for up to 500,000 folds, and the phone has IP68/IP69 water resistance, which is uncommon on foldables. The price went up about 10% from the Magic V5's RM 6,999 launch price, so it is a luxury purchase. Most people will get better value from the Magic8 Pro unless they really want the large inner screen.

4. Honor 600 — Best Value Honor

RM 2,599 (12GB + 512GB)

The standard Honor 600 shares the 6.57-inch 1.5K OLED display and design of the Pro, but uses a different chip: the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 instead of the 8 Elite. It also lacks the Pro's 50W wireless charging. That means it is a strong upper mid-range phone rather than a flagship-class performer.

It comes only as 12GB + 512GB at RM 2,599. Compared with the 600 Pro at the same 512GB size (RM 3,299), you save RM 700, which is a fair trade if you don't play heavy games or need wireless charging.

❓ What Malaysian Buyers Ask Most About Honor Phones

These are the questions buyers ask us most often. Real battery hours and game frame rates depend on settings, signal and software updates, so the answers below compare chipsets, battery capacity and charging speed from Honor's published specs rather than quoting lab hours.

Can I play heavy games like Genshin Impact or Honkai: Star Rail on an Honor phone?

On the Magic8 Pro, Magic V6 and 600 Pro, yes. They use Snapdragon 8 Elite chips, the fastest Qualcomm range, and are the best Honor choices for heavy games. The Honor 600 (Snapdragon 7 Gen 4) handles most popular games well, but expect to lower settings in the most demanding titles. The X9e Pro (Snapdragon 6 Gen 5) and X9d 5G (Snapdragon 6 Gen 4) are mid-range chips: fine for Mobile Legends and PUBG Mobile, but use medium settings for heavy games.

How long does the battery last on a Honor phone?

It depends on capacity first. The X9e Pro has the biggest at 11,000 mAh, then the X9d 5G (8,300 mAh), Magic8 Pro (7,100 mAh), 600 Pro and 600 (7,000 mAh) and Magic V6 (6,660 mAh). For normal use a larger battery generally means longer between charges, though screen size, 5G signal and brightness change the result. If battery life is your top priority, choose the X9e Pro or X9d 5G.

Is the Honor camera good?

The Magic8 Pro has the strongest camera in the lineup: a 50MP main camera, a 200MP periscope telephoto with 3.7x optical zoom, and a 50MP ultrawide. The Magic V6 also has a 64MP periscope telephoto, and the 600 Pro has a 200MP camera system. The X9e Pro has a 108MP main camera with CIPA 6.0 stabilisation, which is good for everyday photos. For the best photos in low light and at zoom, go for the Magic8 Pro.

How fast can an Honor phone charge?

The Magic8 Pro supports up to 100W wired and 80W wireless charging. The 600 Pro and X9e Pro both support 80W wired (the 600 Pro adds 50W wireless), and the Magic V6 supports 80W wired and 66W wireless. The X9d 5G charges at 66W. These are the maximum rated speeds with the original charger, and real times vary with battery size. Bigger batteries such as the X9e Pro's 11,000 mAh take longer to fill than a 5,000 mAh phone even at the same wattage.

How long can I game on one charge?

We don't have a single fixed number, because it depends on the game, graphics settings and brightness. Heavy 3D games drain a battery much faster than light games, often in just a few hours on any phone. As a rule, a bigger battery and a more efficient chip give longer gaming time. The X9e Pro's 11,000 mAh battery is the best on paper for long sessions, and the Snapdragon 8 Elite phones are better for high settings. Keep the phone cool and the screen brightness moderate to get more time.

What is the difference between the Honor 600 and Honor 600 Pro?

The Pro uses the Snapdragon 8 Elite chip and adds 50W wireless charging. The standard 600 uses the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 and has no wireless charging. Both have a 6.57-inch OLED display. In Malaysia the 600 costs RM 2,599 (12GB + 512GB) and the Pro costs RM 3,099 (256GB) or RM 3,299 (512GB).
