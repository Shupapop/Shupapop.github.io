Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 to Gen 5 — Which Is Best for Gaming? (2026)

Published by SpecShelf · 2026 · Malaysia Edition

Qualcomm's Snapdragon 6 series sits right in the sweet spot of the mid-range market — affordable enough for everyday buyers, powerful enough for serious mobile gaming. From Gen 1 in 2022 all the way to the brand new Gen 5 in 2026, each generation has brought meaningful improvements.

But which one is actually the best for gaming? And which phones in Malaysia use each chipset? Let's break it all down.

Quick Ranking — Best to Good

Rank Chipset Year AnTuTu Score GPU
🥇 1st Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 2024 ~868,000 Adreno 710
🥈 2nd Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 2022 ~846,000 Adreno 710
🥉 3rd Snapdragon 6 Gen 4 2025 ~764,000 Adreno 810
4th Snapdragon 6 Gen 2 2023 ~720,000 Adreno 710
5th Snapdragon 6 Gen 5 2026 TBA Adreno (new)

*AnTuTu scores are approximate based on real device testing. Snapdragon 6 Gen 5 benchmarks not yet widely published as of September 2026.

🥇 1. Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 — Best for Gaming Overall

Released: September 2024  |  Process: 4nm  |  GPU: Adreno 710  |  AnTuTu: ~868,000

The Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 is the top gaming performer in the 6-series right now. It features a faster CPU clocked at up to 2.4GHz, the same capable Adreno 710 GPU as Gen 1 but better optimised, and strong support for LPDDR5 memory. In real-world gaming — PUBG Mobile, Mobile Legends, Genshin Impact — it handles high settings smoothly with minimal frame drops.

Phones in Malaysia using this chip: Xiaomi Redmi Note 15, Samsung Galaxy A36 5G, POCO M8

  • ✅ Fastest CPU in the 6-series
  • ✅ Great GPU for mid-range gaming
  • ✅ LPDDR5 RAM support
  • ❌ Slightly lower GPU score than Gen 4 in some tests

🥈 2. Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 — The Original Powerhouse

Released: September 2022  |  Process: 4nm  |  GPU: Adreno 710  |  AnTuTu: ~846,000

It's impressive that the Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 from 2022 still holds up so well in 2026. Built on 4nm with the Adreno 710 GPU, it was a massive leap when it launched and still delivers a smooth gaming experience. Many popular mid-range phones in Malaysia use this chip and they remain excellent daily drivers for gaming today.

Phones in Malaysia using this chip: Honor X9b, OPPO Reno 12 Pro, vivo V30e, Realme 12 Pro

  • ✅ Strong and proven GPU performance
  • ✅ Widely available in popular phones
  • ✅ 4nm efficiency — good battery life
  • ❌ Older generation — fewer AI features

🥉 3. Snapdragon 6 Gen 4 — Best GPU of the Bunch

Released: February 2025  |  Process: 4nm (TSMC)  |  GPU: Adreno 810  |  AnTuTu: ~764,000

The Snapdragon 6 Gen 4 is interesting — its overall AnTuTu score is lower than Gen 1 and Gen 3, but its GPU (Adreno 810) is actually newer and better for graphics rendering. It also has the best multi-core CPU score in the 6-series and is the first to use ARMv9 CPU cores. For graphics-heavy games like Genshin Impact or Asphalt Legends, the Adreno 810 shines. It's also manufactured by TSMC — generally better for thermal efficiency.

Phones in Malaysia using this chip: Honor X9d, vivo Y300 5G

  • ✅ Best GPU in the 6-series (Adreno 810)
  • ✅ ARMv9 architecture — future ready
  • ✅ TSMC 4nm — better thermals
  • ❌ Lower overall AnTuTu than Gen 1 and Gen 3

4. Snapdragon 6 Gen 2 — The Quiet Middle Child

Released: 2023  |  Process: 4nm  |  GPU: Adreno 710  |  AnTuTu: ~720,000

The Snapdragon 6 Gen 2 sits between Gen 1 and Gen 3 but doesn't offer a dramatic improvement over Gen 1. It's a solid chipset for everyday gaming — PUBG Mobile and Mobile Legends run well at medium-high settings — but if you're choosing between a Gen 2 and Gen 1 phone at the same price, the Gen 1's higher benchmark scores give it a slight edge.

  • ✅ Solid mid-range performance
  • ✅ 4nm efficiency
  • ❌ Not a big step up from Gen 1
  • ❌ Less common in Malaysian market

5. Snapdragon 6 Gen 5 — The New Kid (Watch This Space)

Released: May 2026  |  Process: 4nm  |  GPU: Adreno (new gen)  |  AnTuTu: Not yet published

The Snapdragon 6 Gen 5 is brand new as of mid-2026 and full benchmark data is not yet widely available. Early specs show a faster CPU clocked at up to 2.6GHz — the fastest in the 6-series — with the latest Adreno GPU. It runs on LPDDR5 RAM and promises meaningful AI and gaming improvements over Gen 4. We expect it to top this ranking once real-world gaming benchmarks come in. Watch this space.

  • ✅ Fastest CPU clock in the 6-series (2.6GHz)
  • ✅ Latest Adreno GPU
  • ✅ LPDDR5 support
  • ⏳ Full gaming benchmarks not yet available

Which Should You Buy in Malaysia?

Here's the simple answer:

  • Best gaming right now: Go for a phone with Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 — highest overall score and widely available.
  • Best GPU for graphics-heavy games: Snapdragon 6 Gen 4 (Honor X9d, vivo Y300 5G).
  • Best value: Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 phones are now cheaper and still perform excellently.
  • Most future-proof: Wait for Snapdragon 6 Gen 5 phones to arrive in Malaysia — expected late 2026.

All of these chipsets can handle PUBG Mobile, Mobile Legends, and Free Fire at high settings. The difference shows in more demanding titles like Genshin Impact or Honkai: Star Rail, where Gen 3 and Gen 4 pull ahead.
