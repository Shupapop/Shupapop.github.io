Can Samsung phones handle PUBG, Mobile Legends and Genshin Impact?

This is one of the questions we hear most. Below is our honest answer based on each phone's chip, screen and battery. These are estimates from specifications and general user reports, not results from our own lab tests, so real results can vary by game version, settings and room temperature.

Will it run the games smoothly?
Samsung's S-series flagships (S26 Ultra, S26+, S26 and the S25 range) have powerful chips and can run PUBG Mobile, Mobile Legends and Genshin Impact at high settings. The Galaxy A56 and A36 are good for PUBG Mobile and Mobile Legends at medium to high settings, but Genshin Impact is best with lower graphics. The Galaxy A16 5G is fine for Mobile Legends and casual games, but expect lower settings and some drops in heavy fights.

Does it get hot or lag when you play for a long time?
All phones warm up during long sessions. Flagships usually manage heat better because they have larger cooling systems, but mid-range phones may slow down slightly after a long session. To reduce heat, play without a thick case, lower the graphics a little, avoid charging while gaming, and use Samsung's built-in Game Booster.

How long does the battery last when gaming?
Most Samsung phones in this guide have a 5,000mAh battery or close to it. As a rough guide, heavy gaming usually drains a phone of this size in about four to six hours, depending on graphics, brightness and refresh rate. The S26 Ultra supports 60W charging and the A56 supports 45W, so a quick top-up between games is easy.

SpecShelf gaming pick

For serious gaming, choose an S-series flagship. If your budget is limited, the Galaxy A56 5G is the best Samsung for Mobile Legends and PUBG Mobile. The A36 is a cheaper option with the same chip, and the A16 is only for casual games.
