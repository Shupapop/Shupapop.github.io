The best Samsung phones in Malaysia 2026

Best Overall

Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra

From RM 5,699 (256GB) · RM 6,299 (512GB) · RM 6,999 (1TB)
"The most powerful Android phone you can buy in Malaysia."

The S25 Ultra is Samsung's best — full stop. Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset, a 200MP main camera, built-in S Pen, and a 6.9-inch Dynamic AMOLED display that is genuinely stunning. If you want the absolute best Android experience and the budget is there, this is it.

The S Pen alone separates it from every other phone on this list. It's built into the body, always available, and useful for notes, sketches, and precise screen control. Battery life is solid at 5,000mAh, and Samsung promises 7 years of OS and security updates — one of the best software commitments on any Android phone.

Runner-Up

Samsung Galaxy S25+

From RM 4,599 (256GB) · RM 5,099 (512GB)
"All the S25 Ultra power, without the S Pen premium."

If RM 5,699 is too much but you still want a true flagship, the S25+ is the answer. Same Snapdragon 8 Elite chip as the Ultra, same 7 years of updates, same beautiful AMOLED display — just no S Pen and a 50MP main camera instead of 200MP.

The 6.7-inch screen and 4,900mAh battery make this a proper all-day powerhouse. For most people, the S25+ is actually the smarter buy — you get 90% of the Ultra experience for RM 1,100 less.

Top Pick

Samsung Galaxy S25

From RM 3,799 (128GB) · RM 4,099 (256GB)
"The flagship for people who hate big phones."

The standard S25 is the one to get if you find large phones uncomfortable. At 6.2 inches it's compact by modern standards, but it still runs the same Snapdragon 8 Elite chip as the Ultra and S25+. You're not getting a downgraded experience — just a smaller body.

Camera is excellent (50MP main, 12MP ultrawide, 10MP 3x telephoto), battery is 4,000mAh which is fine for the smaller size, and the build quality is premium glass and aluminium. At RM 3,799 it's the entry point to Samsung's flagship lineup — and a very capable one.

SpecShelf take — S-series

All three S25 models share the same chip and software commitment. The choice is really about size and whether the S Pen justifies RM 1,100 extra. For most Malaysian buyers, the S25+ is the sweet spot — big battery, big screen, no compromise on power.

Best Mid-Range

Samsung Galaxy A56 5G

From RM 1,999 (8GB/256GB)
"The best Samsung you can buy without spending flagship money."

The Galaxy A56 5G is Samsung's most important phone for the Malaysian market. It hits the sweet spot — premium enough to feel flagship-adjacent, priced well within reach of most buyers. The 6.7-inch Super AMOLED display is genuinely excellent for the price, and the 50MP main camera produces clean, detailed shots in good light.

It runs Samsung's Exynos 1580 chipset which handles everyday tasks and social media with no issues. Gaming is decent for casual titles, though demanding games like Genshin Impact will need settings turned down. You get 6 years of OS updates — outstanding for a mid-range phone — and the aluminium frame feels solid in hand.

Top Pick

Samsung Galaxy A36 5G

From RM 1,399 (8GB/128GB)
"Great value for Malaysian buyers who want 5G without overspending."

The A36 5G is the best Samsung phone under RM 1,500 right now. It shares the same Exynos 1580 chip as the more expensive A56, which means performance is surprisingly strong for the price. The 6.6-inch Super AMOLED display with 120Hz looks great, and the 5,000mAh battery easily lasts a full day.

Camera is a 50MP main that handles daylight well. Low light is average — don't expect miracles. But for the price, the overall package is hard to fault. Students, first-time smartphone buyers, and anyone wanting a reliable daily driver will be very happy with the A36.

Budget Pick

Samsung Galaxy A16 5G

From RM 799 (4GB/128GB)
"The most affordable way to get a Samsung with 5G and a long software life."

Under RM 800 for a 5G Samsung phone with 6 years of OS updates? That is genuinely impressive. The A16 5G is not fast — its MediaTek Dimensity 6300 chipset is budget-grade — but it handles WhatsApp, social media, YouTube and casual gaming without issues.

The 6.7-inch LCD screen is bright enough for daily use, and the 5,000mAh battery is a strong point. Don't expect great cameras or premium gaming performance. But as a first smartphone, a backup phone, or a device for an older family member, the A16 5G overdelivers at RM 799.

Which Samsung should you buy?

Here's the honest breakdown for Malaysian buyers in 2026:

  • Budget under RM 900: Galaxy A16 5G. 5G, big battery, 6 years of updates. Hard to beat at the price.
  • Budget under RM 1,500: Galaxy A36 5G. Same chip as the A56, better price. Strong all-rounder.
  • Budget under RM 2,000: Galaxy A56 5G. The best mid-range Samsung right now. Worth the extra over A36 if you can stretch.
  • Budget under RM 4,000: Galaxy S25. Flagship chip, compact size, premium build. No compromises.
  • Budget under RM 5,000: Galaxy S25+. The best all-round flagship. Most people should pick this over the Ultra.
  • Want the very best: Galaxy S25 Ultra. S Pen, 200MP camera, the most powerful Samsung made. If budget is not the issue, this is the one.
SpecShelf final verdict

Samsung's 2026 lineup is strong at every price point. The A56 5G is the standout value pick for most Malaysian buyers — it gives you a premium display, a capable camera, 5G, and 6 years of updates for RM 1,999. If you can stretch to the flagship tier, the S25+ is the sweet spot of the S25 range. And if you only have RM 799 to spend, the A16 5G is a genuine surprise at that price.
