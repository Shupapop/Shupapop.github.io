Best Budget 5G Phones Under RM 1,500 in Malaysia (2026)

Published by SpecShelf · Updated 2026 · Malaysia Edition

5G is no longer a luxury in Malaysia. In 2026, you can get a solid 5G smartphone for under RM 1,500 — with a great display, long battery life, and a capable camera. Whether you're a student, a working adult on a budget, or just looking to upgrade without overspending, this list is for you.

We've picked the best options available in Malaysia right now, based on value for money, real-world performance, and what matters most to everyday users.

1. Xiaomi Redmi Note 14 Pro+ — Best Overall Under RM 1,500

Price: RM 1,599 (frequently on sale under RM 1,500)

  • 6.67-inch AMOLED display, 120Hz
  • Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 processor
  • 200MP main camera
  • 6,000mAh battery with 90W fast charging
  • 5G connectivity

The Redmi Note 14 Pro+ punches way above its price. The 200MP camera is a headline feature, but what really impresses is the massive 6,000mAh battery that easily lasts two days. Fast charging at 90W means you're never waiting long. For anyone who wants the most phone for the least money, this is the one to beat.

2. Samsung Galaxy A35 5G — Best for Samsung Fans

Price: RM 1,399

  • 6.6-inch Super AMOLED display, 120Hz
  • Exynos 1380 processor
  • 50MP main camera
  • 5,000mAh battery, 25W charging
  • 5G connectivity
  • IP67 water resistance

Samsung's Galaxy A35 5G is the safe, reliable choice. It has one of the best displays in this price range — the Super AMOLED panel is bright, vibrant, and smooth. IP67 water resistance is a rare bonus under RM 1,500. If you want a trusted brand with long software support, the A35 5G delivers.

3. vivo Y300 5G — Best Battery Life

Price: RM 1,099

  • 6.67-inch AMOLED display, 120Hz
  • Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 processor
  • 50MP main camera
  • 5,000mAh battery, 44W fast charging
  • 5G connectivity

At RM 1,099, the vivo Y300 5G is one of the most affordable 5G phones in Malaysia with a proper AMOLED screen. It's slim, light, and the 44W charging is faster than most competitors at this price. A great pick if you want 5G on a tight budget without sacrificing display quality.

4. OPPO Reno 12 Pro — Best Camera Under RM 1,500

Price: RM 1,999 (watch for promotions bringing it under RM 1,500)

  • 6.7-inch AMOLED display, 120Hz
  • MediaTek Dimensity 7300 Energy processor
  • 50MP main + 50MP portrait camera
  • 5,000mAh battery, 80W fast charging
  • 5G connectivity

The OPPO Reno 12 Pro is a camera-focused phone with a dual 50MP setup that produces excellent portraits. The 80W fast charging is class-leading at this segment. Keep an eye out for Lazada or Shopee sales — it frequently drops into the RM 1,500 range during campaigns.

Final Verdict

If you can only pick one, go with the Xiaomi Redmi Note 14 Pro+ — it has the best specs-to-price ratio in this list. For those who prefer Samsung's ecosystem and software reliability, the Galaxy A35 5G is a solid runner-up. On the tightest budget, the vivo Y300 5G at RM 1,099 is genuinely impressive.

5G coverage in Malaysia is expanding fast — especially in Klang Valley, Penang, and Johor Bahru. Getting a 5G phone now means you're ready for the network as it grows nationwide.
