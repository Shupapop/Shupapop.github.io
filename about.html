About SpecShelf

A phone specs site built for Malaysian buyers, by someone who actually buys phones in Malaysia.

SpecShelf started because of a frustration most Malaysian phone buyers will recognise: you find a phone you like, you Google the specs, and you end up on a site with prices in USD, storage options that aren't sold here, and zero mention of where to actually buy it in Malaysia.

We built SpecShelf to fix that. Full specs, real MYR pricing, and buying guides written for the Malaysian market — not translated from somewhere else and not copied from the manufacturer's press release.

What we do

SpecShelf covers phones and tablets available in Malaysia. We publish full specification sheets, side-by-side comparisons, and editorial buying guides — all with Malaysian Ringgit pricing and local market context.

Our specs come from official manufacturer sources only. Our prices are based on official brand Malaysia listings and are updated regularly, though we always note that retail prices vary and recommend checking current prices before buying.

How we pick what to review

We focus on phones that are actually sold in Malaysia — officially, through authorised channels. We don't cover grey-import-only devices unless there's a strong reason to. And we try to cover every budget, not just flagships — because most Malaysian buyers are shopping between RM 800 and RM 2,500, not at the RM 5,000+ tier.

Advertising & independence

SpecShelf earns revenue through Google AdSense ads and occasional affiliate links. These help keep the site free and running. Our editorial recommendations are completely independent — we are not paid by any phone brand to feature or promote their products. The ads you see on SpecShelf are served automatically by Google and have no influence on our content.
