Best iPhone in Malaysia 2026 — Every Model Ranked

Apple's 2026 iPhone lineup is the most interesting in years. For the first time, Apple has launched a foldable iPhone, the iPhone Duo, alongside the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max. There is no standard iPhone 18 this year, so the iPhone 17 and iPhone 17e remain the entry-level models, and the older iPhone 16 and 15 are still popular with buyers who want to save money.

For Malaysian buyers, pricing is always the challenge. iPhones cost more here than in the US or Singapore due to import duties and SST, and Apple raised prices on several models in September 2026. This guide covers every iPhone worth considering in Malaysia, from the newest Duo down to the iPhone 15, with prices checked against Apple Malaysia and authorised resellers.

Every iPhone in Malaysia 2026 — ranked

NEW — Foldable

Apple iPhone Duo

From RM 9,499 (256GB) · RM 10,999 (512GB) · RM 12,999 (1TB) · RM 15,499 (2TB)
"Apple’s first foldable iPhone — and one of the most significant iPhone launches in years."

The iPhone Duo is Apple’s answer to Samsung’s Galaxy Z Fold. When closed it is a 5.4-inch smartphone. Open it up and you get a 7.6-inch tablet-sized display, the largest screen Apple has ever put in an iPhone. The inner display uses self-healing memory glass and an under-display FaceTime camera that disappears when not in use.

It runs the same A20 Pro chip as the iPhone 18 Pro, so performance is flagship-class throughout. The 7,500mAh battery is the largest Apple has put in an iPhone, with 80W wired and wireless fast charging. Apple Pencil works on the inner display, which makes it useful for note-taking and sketching.

At RM 9,499 it is unquestionably expensive. Pre-orders open 16 October with availability from 23 October in Malaysia. It is eSIM-only, with no physical SIM.

Best Overall

Apple iPhone 18 Pro Max

From RM 5,999 (256GB) · RM 6,999 (512GB) · RM 8,999 (1TB) · RM 11,999 (2TB)
"The best conventional iPhone you can buy — A20 Pro, best-ever battery life and the full Pro camera system."

The iPhone 18 Pro Max is Apple’s best non-folding iPhone and a big upgrade over the 17 Pro Max. The A20 Pro chip, built on a 2nm process, delivers much higher sustained performance, but the headline improvement is battery life: Apple rates it at up to 43 hours of video playback, the longest of any Pro iPhone.

The 6.9-inch ProMotion display is the best screen Apple has made, and the triple camera system, with variable aperture on the main lens, gives photographers more control than any previous iPhone. It ships with iOS 27. At RM 5,999 for 256GB it is RM 3,500 cheaper than the iPhone Duo while delivering the full Pro experience.

Runner-Up

Apple iPhone 18 Pro

From RM 5,499 (256GB) · RM 6,499 (512GB) · RM 8,499 (1TB) · RM 11,499 (2TB)
"Same A20 Pro chip and Pro cameras in a more comfortable size for RM 500 less."

The iPhone 18 Pro shares the A20 Pro chip and the full camera system with the Pro Max, including the variable aperture main lens. The 6.3-inch display is smaller but equally good, and battery life is rated at up to 34 hours. For most buyers who do not specifically need the biggest screen, the 18 Pro is the smarter choice: easier to use one-handed, more pocketable and RM 500 cheaper.

Both 18 Pro models are available in Malaysia from 18 September 2026. They support eSIM and a physical SIM, unlike the eSIM-only iPhone Duo.

SpecShelf take — iPhone 18 Pro vs Pro Max

Both share the same A20 Pro chip, the same camera system and the same iOS 27 features. The only real differences are screen size and battery. Pick the Pro Max for the biggest screen and longest battery. Pick the Pro if you want a more manageable size and want to save RM 500. For most people, the Pro is the better daily phone.

Thinnest

Apple iPhone Air

From RM 5,299 (256GB) · RM 6,299 (512GB) · RM 8,299 (1TB)
"Apple’s thinnest iPhone — a large display in a remarkably slim and light body."

The iPhone Air is unlike any other iPhone. It is the slimmest iPhone Apple has made, with a 6.5-inch display in a body that feels noticeably lighter than the Pro models. It runs the A19 Pro chip, so performance is strong for everyday use and gaming.

The single 48MP rear camera is a step down from the Pro models, with no telephoto lens, but image quality is still very good. The Air is for buyers who put design first. Note that Malaysian prices rose by RM 300 (and RM 1,300 for the 1TB model) in September 2026 after the iPhone 18 Pro launch.

Best Standard

Apple iPhone 17

From RM 4,299 (256GB) · RM 5,299 (512GB)
"The standard iPhone for most people — current chip, full Apple Intelligence and a bright 120Hz display."

The iPhone 17 is the main entry point to Apple’s current generation and now starts at RM 4,299 for 256GB after the September 2026 price increase (it was RM 3,999). It has a 6.3-inch ProMotion display, a dual rear camera system and full Apple Intelligence support.

For buyers who want a current-generation iPhone without paying Pro prices, this is the right pick. There is no iPhone 18 standard model this year, so the iPhone 17 stays Apple’s mainstream iPhone until next year.

Cheapest New

Apple iPhone 17e

From RM 3,299 (256GB) · RM 4,299 (512GB)
"The cheapest new iPhone Apple sells in Malaysia — a way into iOS without the Pro price."

The iPhone 17e is Apple’s most affordable current iPhone, with a 6.1-inch OLED display and a single 48MP Fusion camera. It supports Apple Intelligence and 5G. Its price went up by RM 300 in September 2026, so it now starts at RM 3,299 rather than RM 2,999.

You give up the 120Hz ProMotion display and the extra camera of the iPhone 17, but for first-time iPhone buyers, students or Android switchers, it is the cheapest way to get a new iPhone with full Apple warranty.

Older iPhones still worth buying in Malaysia

If the new prices are too high, the previous generations are still solid. They are no longer sold at the official Apple Malaysia price points, so stock and prices depend on authorised resellers and promotions. Always compare a few sellers and check the warranty before you buy.

"The previous-generation Pro iPhones remain excellent, especially with a discount."

The iPhone 16 Pro and 16 Pro Max still have a ProMotion display, a telephoto camera and Apple Intelligence support, and they will keep getting iOS updates for years. If you can find one at a good discount from an authorised seller, it is the best way to get Pro features without paying 18 Pro prices.

Apple iPhone 16 / iPhone 15

"A step below the iPhone 17 — the discount is what makes them worth it."

The iPhone 16 runs the A18 chip, supports Apple Intelligence and has the Action button. An authorised Malaysian reseller was listing it from about RM 3,799 at the time of writing, which makes it roughly RM 500 cheaper than the iPhone 17 with the same 256GB base now at RM 4,299, so it only makes sense if you do not need the 120Hz display.

Which iPhone should you buy in Malaysia 2026?

  • Cheapest new iPhone: iPhone 17e at RM 3,299. Good for first-time buyers or switching from Android.
  • Best standard iPhone: iPhone 17 at RM 4,299. Current chip, 120Hz display, Apple Intelligence, solid cameras.
  • Want the thinnest iPhone: iPhone Air at RM 5,299. Large screen, very light, but a single rear camera.
  • Want the full Pro experience: iPhone 18 Pro at RM 5,499. A20 Pro chip, variable aperture camera and a comfortable size.
  • Want the best battery and biggest screen: iPhone 18 Pro Max at RM 5,999.
  • Want something genuinely new: iPhone Duo at RM 9,499, Apple’s first foldable.
  • On a tighter budget: a discounted iPhone 16 Pro or iPhone 16 from an authorised reseller.
SpecShelf final verdict

For most Malaysian buyers the iPhone 18 Pro at RM 5,499 is the best all-round iPhone, and the iPhone 17 at RM 4,299 is the sensible choice if you do not need Pro features. The iPhone Duo at RM 9,499 is exciting but a niche purchase. If you want to save money, a discounted iPhone 16 Pro from an authorised reseller often beats a full-price iPhone 17e.
