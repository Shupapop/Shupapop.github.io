Best iPhone in Malaysia 2026 — Every Model Ranked

iPhone 16 Pro Max down to iPhone 15 — we rank every iPhone worth buying in Malaysia right now, with real MYR prices and straight verdicts for every budget.

Apple iPhones are among the most searched phones in Malaysia — and for good reason. iOS is polished, performance is class-leading, and the camera quality across the entire lineup is exceptional. The challenge for Malaysian buyers is the price: iPhones are significantly more expensive here than in the US or Singapore due to import duties and SST.

This guide ranks every current iPhone available in Malaysia, from the top-of-the-line iPhone 16 Pro Max down to the iPhone 15 — which remains excellent value at a reduced price now that the 16 series is out. Real MYR prices, honest verdicts, no fluff.

The best iPhones in Malaysia 2026

Best Overall

Apple iPhone 16 Pro Max

From RM 6,299 (256GB) · RM 7,099 (512GB) · RM 7,899 (1TB)
"The most powerful iPhone ever made — and the best smartphone camera you can buy in Malaysia."

The iPhone 16 Pro Max is Apple's best, and it shows. The A18 Pro chip is faster than any Android chipset available today for sustained performance. The camera system — 48MP main, 12MP ultrawide, 12MP 5x periscope telephoto — produces images and 4K video that set the benchmark for smartphone photography. Apple Intelligence AI features are built in, with the Action Button and Camera Control adding genuine utility.

The 6.9-inch Super Retina XDR ProMotion display is the best screen Apple has ever put in an iPhone. Battery life is excellent — easily a full day of heavy use, often pushing into a second. At RM 6,299 it's expensive by any measure, but for buyers who want the absolute best smartphone experience regardless of platform, this is the one to get.

Runner-Up

Apple iPhone 16 Pro

From RM 5,399 (128GB) · RM 5,999 (256GB) · RM 7,199 (1TB)
"Same Pro chip, same Pro cameras — in a more comfortable compact size."

The iPhone 16 Pro shares the same A18 Pro chip and the same triple camera system as the Pro Max — including the 5x periscope telephoto. The only meaningful differences are the smaller 6.3-inch display and a smaller battery. For most people, the Pro is actually the better choice — it's easier to use one-handed, more pocketable, and RM 900 cheaper.

If you don't specifically need the largest screen, the iPhone 16 Pro is the smarter buy in Apple's Pro lineup. You get the full Pro camera experience, the full A18 Pro performance, and the Action Button and Camera Control — in a phone that's noticeably easier to carry every day.

SpecShelf take — iPhone 16 Pro vs Pro Max

Both share the same chip and cameras — the choice is purely about size and battery. Pro Max is for big-screen fans who want the longest battery life. Pro is for everyone else who wants the full Pro experience in a more practical size. We'd pick the Pro for most buyers.

Top Pick

Apple iPhone 16 Plus

From RM 4,699 (128GB) · RM 5,299 (256GB)
"The iPhone with the best battery life — and a big screen without the Pro price."

The iPhone 16 Plus runs the standard A18 chip rather than the A18 Pro, and uses a dual camera system (48MP main + 12MP ultrawide) without the periscope telephoto. But it has something the Pro Max doesn't — arguably better battery life for everyday use because the larger battery powers fewer demanding camera features.

The 6.7-inch Super Retina XDR display is beautiful, and the A18 chip is still extremely fast for any task. If you want a large iPhone, great battery life, and a price below RM 5,000 — and you don't need the telephoto zoom or ProMotion 120Hz — the iPhone 16 Plus is the right call.

Top Pick

Apple iPhone 16

From RM 3,999 (128GB) · RM 4,599 (256GB)
"The entry point to the iPhone 16 family — A18 chip and a great camera for under RM 4,000."

The iPhone 16 is the most accessible new iPhone in Malaysia right now. At RM 3,999 for the 128GB model, you get the A18 chip, a 48MP main camera with significant computational photography improvements over the iPhone 15, the Action Button, and the new Camera Control button. Apple Intelligence features are fully supported.

The 6.1-inch display is excellent — bright, accurate, and easy to read outdoors — though it's limited to 60Hz unlike the Pro models. Battery life is good for a full day of normal use. For Malaysian buyers who want a current-generation iPhone at the lowest possible entry price, the iPhone 16 delivers the full modern iPhone experience without the flagship premium.

Best Value

Apple iPhone 15

From RM 3,299 (128GB) · RM 3,799 (256GB)
"Still an excellent iPhone — and now the best value Apple in Malaysia."

With the iPhone 16 series now available, the iPhone 15 has dropped in price and become the best value iPhone you can buy in Malaysia. The A16 Bionic chip is still very fast — faster than most Android flagships — and the 48MP main camera produces excellent photos and 4K video. Dynamic Island, USB-C charging, and the premium titanium-framed design are all here.

What you miss compared to the iPhone 16 is Apple Intelligence support, the Camera Control button, and slightly improved camera processing. For most users, none of those will matter. At RM 3,299, the iPhone 15 gives you a premium, long-lasting iPhone experience at the most accessible price in the current Apple lineup.

Which iPhone should you buy?

Straight advice for Malaysian buyers in 2026:

  • Best value iPhone: iPhone 15 at RM 3,299. Still fast, great camera, premium build. The smart buy if budget matters.
  • Best new iPhone under RM 4,000: iPhone 16 at RM 3,999. Current-gen chip, Apple Intelligence, Camera Control. Worth the extra RM 700 over the 15 if you want the latest.
  • Want a big screen without Pro money: iPhone 16 Plus at RM 4,699. Best battery life in the non-Pro lineup.
  • Want the full Pro experience: iPhone 16 Pro at RM 5,399. Same chip and cameras as the Pro Max in a more practical size.
  • Want the absolute best: iPhone 16 Pro Max at RM 6,299. Biggest screen, longest battery, best camera. The flagship of flagships.
SpecShelf final verdict

For most Malaysian buyers, the iPhone 15 at RM 3,299 is the sweet spot — you get a premium Apple experience at the lowest current price without sacrificing anything essential. If you want current-gen and can stretch to RM 3,999, the iPhone 16 adds Apple Intelligence and the new Camera Control. And if cameras are everything to you, the iPhone 16 Pro at RM 5,399 is genuinely one of the best smartphone cameras on the market — regardless of brand.
