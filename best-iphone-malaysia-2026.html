NEW — Foldable

Apple iPhone Duo

From RM 9,499 (256GB) · RM 10,999 (512GB) · RM 12,999 (1TB) · RM 15,499 (2TB)
"Apple's first foldable iPhone — and one of the most significant product launches in iPhone history."

The iPhone Duo is Apple's answer to Samsung's Galaxy Z Fold — and it's a genuinely compelling device. When closed, it's a 5.4-inch smartphone. Open it up and you have a 7.6-inch tablet-sized display, currently the largest screen Apple has ever put in an iPhone. The inner display uses self-healing memory glass and has an under-display FaceTime camera that disappears when not in use.

It runs the same A20 Pro chip found in the iPhone 18 Pro, which means performance is flagship-class throughout. The 7,500mAh battery is Apple's largest ever in an iPhone, and 80W wired and wireless fast charging is significantly faster than previous iPhones. Apple Pencil support works on the inner display, making this genuinely useful as a tablet replacement for note-taking and sketching.

At RM 9,499 it is unquestionably expensive. Pre-orders opened 16 October with availability from 23 October in Malaysia. eSIM-only — no physical SIM. Supported carriers include CelcomDigi, Maxis, U Mobile and Yes.

Best Overall

Apple iPhone 18 Pro Max

From RM 5,999 (256GB) · RM 6,999 (512GB) · RM 8,999 (1TB) · RM 11,999 (2TB)
"The best conventional iPhone you can buy — A20 Pro, best-ever battery life, and the full Pro camera system."

The iPhone 18 Pro Max is Apple's best conventional (non-folding) iPhone, and it's a significant upgrade over the 17 Pro Max. The A20 Pro chip, built on a 2nm process, delivers up to 40% higher sustained performance than the A19 Pro. But the headline improvement is battery life — Apple rates the 18 Pro Max at up to 43 hours of video playback, making it the longest-lasting Pro iPhone ever.

The 6.9-inch Super Retina XDR ProMotion display is the best screen Apple has made, and the triple camera system — with variable aperture on the main lens — gives photographers more creative control than any previous iPhone. The 18 Pro Max ships with iOS 27 out of the box. At RM 5,999 for 256GB, it's RM 1,000 cheaper than the iPhone Duo while delivering the full Pro iPhone experience.

Runner-Up

Apple iPhone 18 Pro

From RM 5,499 (256GB) · RM 6,499 (512GB) · RM 8,499 (1TB) · RM 11,499 (2TB)
"Same A20 Pro chip and Pro cameras — in a more comfortable compact size for RM 500 less."

The iPhone 18 Pro shares the A20 Pro chip and the full Pro camera system with the Pro Max, including the variable aperture main lens. The 6.3-inch display is smaller but equally stunning, and battery life is rated at up to 34 hours — still excellent for a full day of heavy use. For most buyers who don't specifically need the largest screen, the 18 Pro is the smarter choice — easier to use one-handed, more pocketable, and RM 500 less expensive.

The iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max are available in Malaysia from 18 September 2026. Both models are eSIM capable but also support a physical SIM — unlike the iPhone Duo which is eSIM only.

SpecShelf take — iPhone 18 Pro vs Pro Max

Both share the same A20 Pro chip, the same camera system with variable aperture, and the same iOS 27 features. The only difference is screen size and battery. Pick the Pro Max if you want the biggest screen and longest battery. Pick the Pro if you prefer a more manageable size and want to save RM 500. For most people, the Pro is the better daily phone.

Top Pick

Apple iPhone Air

From RM 5,299 (256GB) · RM 6,299 (512GB) · RM 8,299 (1TB)
"Apple's thinnest iPhone ever — a large display in a remarkably slim and light body."

The iPhone Air is unlike any other iPhone. At its thinnest point it's the slimmest iPhone Apple has ever made, with a large 6.6-inch display in a body that feels noticeably lighter than either Pro model. It runs Apple's A18 chip (not the Pro variant) which is still very fast for all everyday tasks and gaming.

The single rear camera is a step down from the Pro models — no telephoto, no variable aperture — but image quality from the 48MP main lens is still excellent. The Air is for buyers who prioritise form factor above everything else — if you want the biggest iPhone screen in the thinnest possible body and don't need the Pro camera system, this is the one. Note that prices increased by RM 300 in September 2026 following the iPhone 18 Pro launch.

Top Pick

Apple iPhone 17

From RM 4,299 (128GB) · RM 4,899 (256GB)
"The most accessible new-generation iPhone — full Apple Intelligence support at the lowest Pro-adjacent price."

The iPhone 17 is the entry point to Apple's current iPhone generation. It runs the A18 chip, supports the full Apple Intelligence feature set, and carries a 48MP main camera with a redesigned aluminium-and-glass build that feels noticeably more premium than its predecessor. The 6.1-inch display is crisp and bright, and battery life covers a full day comfortably.

For Malaysian buyers who want a current-generation iPhone under RM 5,000 — without going for the stripped-down 17e — the iPhone 17 is the right pick. You get the current chip, current software, and a camera that handles everyday photography and video very well.

Best Value

Apple iPhone 17e

From RM 2,999 (128GB) · RM 3,499 (256GB)
"The most affordable iPhone in Malaysia — and the entry point to the Apple ecosystem."

The iPhone 17e is Apple's budget iPhone and the most accessible entry into the Apple ecosystem in Malaysia at RM 2,999. It runs the A16 Bionic chip — fast enough for everything most users do — and has a 48MP main camera that takes excellent photos in good light. The 6.1-inch display is OLED and looks great. Dynamic Island is included.

What you give up: no Apple Intelligence support, no 5G on some variants, and a less premium build than the standard 17. But for first-time iPhone buyers, students, or anyone switching from Android who wants the iPhone experience at the lowest possible price, the 17e is the way in. At RM 2,999 it's the best-value iPhone Apple currently sells in Malaysia.

Which iPhone should you buy in Malaysia 2026?

  • Best value iPhone: iPhone 17e at RM 2,999. The cheapest iPhone in Malaysia — great for first-time buyers or switching from Android.
  • Best new iPhone under RM 4,500: iPhone 17 at RM 4,299. Current chip, Apple Intelligence, solid camera, premium build.
  • Want the thinnest iPhone: iPhone Air at RM 5,299. Large screen, incredibly slim and light — for buyers who prioritise design above all.
  • Want the full Pro experience: iPhone 18 Pro at RM 5,499. A20 Pro chip, variable aperture camera, best-in-class performance. The smart Pro pick.
  • Want the best battery and biggest screen: iPhone 18 Pro Max at RM 5,999. Up to 43 hours of battery, the largest Pro display, the complete flagship.
  • Want something genuinely new: iPhone Duo at RM 9,499. Apple's first foldable — a 7.6-inch inner display, A20 Pro, 7,500mAh battery. The most exciting iPhone in years.
SpecShelf final verdict

Apple's 2026 lineup is the strongest it has been in years. The iPhone 17e at RM 2,999 is the best entry point into the Apple ecosystem. The iPhone 18 Pro at RM 5,499 is the best all-round Pro iPhone for most buyers — same chip as the Pro Max, more manageable size. And the iPhone Duo at RM 9,499 is genuinely exciting as Apple's first foldable — but at nearly RM 10,000 it's a niche purchase, not a recommendation for most Malaysian buyers right now.
