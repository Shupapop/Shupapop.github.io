🔋 Tips to Make Your Phone Battery Last Longer in Malaysia

☀️ Lower screen brightness: Malaysia's outdoor brightness tempts everyone to max out the screen — but this kills battery fast. Use auto brightness instead.

📶 Switch off 5G when not needed: 5G uses more battery than 4G. If you're not streaming or downloading, switch to 4G to save power.

🌙 Use dark mode: On AMOLED screens (most phones today), dark mode can save 15-30% battery life.

📍 Limit background apps: Apps like Grab, Waze, and TikTok running in background drain battery. Close them when not in use.

🔌 Don't charge to 100% every time: Keeping your battery between 20-80% extends its lifespan significantly.
