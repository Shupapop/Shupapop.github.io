This guide ranks the Huawei phones that are officially sold in Malaysia, from the budget Nova 14i to the newest Pura 90s flagships, with Malaysian launch prices in MYR and who each phone is best for.

The Nova 14i is the cheapest of the Nova 14 family and its main selling point is a huge 7,000mAh battery. If you want a phone that comfortably lasts through a long day of social media, videos and chat, this is the safe and affordable choice.

It is a good fit for students and first-time Huawei buyers who care about battery life more than camera quality or premium design.

Huawei Nova 14

RM 1,799 (12GB + 256GB)
"The sweet spot of the range — fast charging, a strong selfie camera and a flat-edge design."

The Nova 14 is the best all-round Huawei for most Malaysian buyers. It has a 50MP front camera for portrait selfies, a 5,500mAh battery and 100W Huawei SuperCharge, which means very quick top-ups. The flat-edge design is also easier to hold with a case.

During the launch promotion, buyers also received a free Huawei Band 10 worth RM 189 while stocks lasted, so check whether a bundle is available when you buy.

Huawei Nova 14 Pro

RM 2,399 (12GB + 512GB)
"The same strong selfie camera and charging as the Nova 14, with double the storage."

The Nova 14 Pro costs RM 2,399 and comes with 12GB of RAM and 512GB of storage. It shares the 50MP front camera, the 5,500mAh battery and 100W charging with the standard Nova 14, so the main upgrade is storage and a more premium feel.

Pick this one if you take lots of photos and videos and do not want to manage storage. At launch it was offered with an early bird voucher and instalment plans, so check Huawei Malaysia for the latest deal.

Huawei Pura 80

RM 3,299 (12GB + 256GB)
"The most affordable way into Huawei’s camera-focused Pura flagship series."

The Pura line is where Huawei puts its best cameras. The standard Pura 80 launched in Malaysia at RM 3,299 with a 50MP main camera, a 1.5MP multi-spectral sensor, a 5,170mAh battery, second-generation Kunlun Glass and a frosted glass flat-edge design. It comes in gold, white and black.

The Pura 80 Pro and Pura 80 Ultra were released earlier in Malaysia for buyers who want even stronger cameras. If photography is your main reason for buying a phone, the Pura range is where Huawei really stands out.

Before you buy a Huawei in Malaysia

  • Apps: Huawei phones use AppGallery and Huawei's own services instead of Google. Many popular apps are available, but check that the banking, e-wallet and work apps you use every day work well.
  • Warranty: Buy from Huawei Malaysia or authorised dealers to keep your local warranty.
  • Promotions: Huawei often gives free gifts, vouchers and instalment plans at launch, so compare deals before paying full price.

Which Huawei should you buy in Malaysia 2026?

  • Tight budget and long battery life: Nova 14i at RM 1,299.
  • Best overall value: Nova 14 at RM 1,799.
  • Need lots of storage: Nova 14 Pro at RM 2,399.
  • Want the best camera: Pura 80 at RM 3,299, or the newer Pura 90s Pro and Pro Max.
SpecShelf final verdict

For most people the Nova 14 at RM 1,799 is the best Huawei to buy in Malaysia: fast charging, a good selfie camera and a fair price. If battery life on a budget matters most, choose the Nova 14i. If photography is the priority, step up to the Pura series. Just make sure you are comfortable with AppGallery before you commit.
