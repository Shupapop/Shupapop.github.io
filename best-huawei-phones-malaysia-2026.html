Best Huawei Phones in Malaysia 2026

Updated October 2026 · By SpecShelf · 6 min read

Huawei phones are a bit different in Malaysia. You get strong cameras, fast charging and good build quality, but you use Huawei's own app ecosystem (AppGallery and HarmonyOS-style features) instead of the usual Google setup. This guide helps you pick the right Huawei phone by budget, based on the official Malaysian lineup.

Quick answer: Best value is the Huawei Nova 14 (RM1,799). Cheapest is the Nova 14i (RM1,299). For the best camera, go for the Pura series.

1. Huawei Nova 14i Budget pick

The cheapest of the Nova 14 family at RM1,299. Its big selling point is the large 7,000mAh battery, so it suits people who just want a phone that lasts two days without worry. If you are a student or a heavy social media user, this is the safe choice.

2. Huawei Nova 14 Best value

At RM1,799 for 12GB+256GB, the Nova 14 is the sweet spot. It has a 50MP front camera for portrait selfies, a 5,500mAh battery and 100W Huawei SuperCharge, so charging is very fast. It has a flat-edge design, which many people prefer for grip and cases. Buyers also got a free Huawei Band 10 during the launch promotion while stocks lasted.

3. Huawei Nova 14 Pro

The Pro costs RM2,399 for 12GB+512GB. It shares the 50MP front camera and the 5,500mAh battery with 100W charging, but gives you double the storage. Pick this if you take lots of photos and videos and do not want to delete things to free up space.

4. Huawei Pura 80 Camera pick

The Pura line is Huawei's camera flagship. The standard Pura 80 launched at RM3,299 (12GB+256GB) with a 50MP main camera, a 5,170mAh battery, frosted glass and a flat-edge design. It is the most affordable way into the Pura series. If photography matters most to you, this is where Huawei really shines.

5. Huawei Pura 90s Pro and Pura 90s Pro Max

Huawei brought its newest Pura 90s Pro and Pura 90s Pro Max to Malaysia on 14 July 2026 as the successors to the Pura 80 series. Because final Malaysian specs and pricing can differ from the China versions, check the official Huawei Malaysia website for the latest price and specs before you decide.

Things to know before buying a Huawei

  • Apps: Huawei uses AppGallery. Many popular apps are available, but check that the apps you use daily (banking, e-wallet, work apps) work well.
  • Warranty: Buy from the official Huawei store or authorised dealers to keep your Malaysian warranty.
  • Promotions: Huawei often gives free gifts, vouchers and instalment plans, so compare before paying full price.

Which one should you buy?

  • Tight budget, long battery: Nova 14i
  • Best overall value: Nova 14
  • Need lots of storage: Nova 14 Pro
  • Want the best camera: Pura 80 or the newer Pura 90s series
