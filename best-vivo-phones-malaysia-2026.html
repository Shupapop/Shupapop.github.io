What Malaysian buyers ask us most about Vivo

We sell phones to Malaysian buyers on Carousell, and the questions about Vivo are very consistent.

1. Is the camera really that good?
Vivo's cameras are tuned with Zeiss, and the X300 series is built around that partnership. The X300 Pro has a 200MP Zeiss telephoto camera and the X300 has a 200MP Zeiss main camera. For most buyers who care about photos and selfies, a Vivo camera is a safe choice. The X300 Pro is the one for serious photography.

2. How long does the battery last?
Recent Vivo phones use large batteries. The V70 has 6,500mAh with 90W charging and the V70 FE has 7,000mAh, so both should comfortably last a full day of heavy use. Real battery life depends on how you use the phone and the screen brightness.

3. How many software updates will it get?
For the Vivo V70, Vivo promises four OS updates and six years of security patches. This is a long support period for a mid-range phone. For other models, check Vivo Malaysia's page before you buy.

4. Can it play heavy games?
The Vivo X300 and X300 Pro have the Dimensity 9500, a flagship chip that can run heavy games like Genshin Impact and PUBG Mobile at high settings. The V70 handles most games well at medium to high settings, and the V70 FE is best for medium settings. This is based on chip and spec expectations, not our own lab tests.

Buying tip from our seller experience

Many customers ask the same four questions: camera, battery, updates and gaming. If you want the best camera, choose the X300 Pro. If you want the best all-round value, the V70 gives long software support and a large battery for around RM 2,000.

Which Vivo should you buy in Malaysia 2026?

The Vivo X300 Pro is the best Vivo for photography, and the V70 is the best all-round pick for most people thanks to its battery and long update promise. Always compare official store prices with authorised sellers before you buy.
