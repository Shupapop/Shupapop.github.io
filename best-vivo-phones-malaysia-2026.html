Vivo has quietly become one of the most interesting phone brands in Malaysia. The X series punches directly at Samsung and Apple with Zeiss-tuned cameras and flagship chipsets, while the V series delivers premium-looking phones at mid-range prices. And the Y series covers budget buyers better than most brands at equivalent price points.

The lineup can be confusing — lots of models with similar names. This guide cuts through it and picks the best Vivo phones available in Malaysia right now, ranked by price, with honest verdicts for every budget.

The best Vivo phones in Malaysia 2026

Best Overall

Vivo X200 Pro

From RM 4,799 (16GB/512GB)
"Zeiss periscope telephoto, Dimensity 9400, and a 6,000mAh battery — the complete flagship package."

The Vivo X200 Pro is the best phone Vivo has ever made, and it competes seriously with Samsung and Apple at a lower price. The standout feature is the Zeiss-tuned periscope telephoto camera — a 200MP sensor with 3.7x optical zoom that produces sharp, detailed images at distance that most phones simply can't match. Portrait shots are exceptional, with natural background separation that rivals dedicated cameras.

It runs MediaTek Dimensity 9400 — one of the most efficient flagship chipsets available in 2026 — which contributes to the genuinely impressive battery life. The 6,000mAh cell with 90W FlashCharge means you're unlikely to run out of power in a day, even with heavy use. The 6.78-inch LTPO AMOLED display is vivid and smooth, adapting between 1Hz and 120Hz automatically. For buyers who want the best Vivo can offer, this is it.

Runner-Up

Vivo X200

From RM 3,499 (12GB/256GB)
"Flagship Dimensity 9400 performance and Zeiss cameras — for RM 1,300 less than the Pro."

The standard X200 shares the same Dimensity 9400 chipset as the Pro and still carries Zeiss-branded cameras — you're not getting a downgraded experience, just a more compact form factor and a standard telephoto instead of the periscope zoom. For most people, this is the smarter buy in the X200 range.

The 6.67-inch AMOLED display is bright and sharp, the 5,800mAh battery is excellent, and 90W charging keeps top-up time short. If long-range photography isn't your priority but you still want a flagship-class Vivo with Zeiss optics, the X200 delivers at a price that makes it genuinely competitive against Samsung and OPPO in the same tier.

Top Pick

Vivo V40 Pro

From RM 2,299 (12GB/256GB)
"Zeiss portrait cameras and a slim premium design at a price the competition can't match."

The V40 Pro is Vivo's most popular mid-flagship in Malaysia, and it's easy to see why. The Zeiss-tuned dual front cameras — yes, dual selfie cameras — make it the best selfie phone in its price bracket by a clear margin. The 50MP rear main camera handles everyday photography well, and Vivo's AI-enhanced portrait modes are genuinely impressive for social media content creators.

Snapdragon 7 Gen 3 provides smooth performance for daily tasks and gaming. The slim design at 7.6mm is one of the thinnest in its class. Battery is 5,500mAh with 80W FlashCharge — solid for all-day use. If you prioritise camera quality, especially for portraits and selfies, over raw chipset performance, the V40 Pro is the best mid-flagship choice in Malaysia right now.

SpecShelf take — Vivo flagship & mid-flagship

The X200 is the strongest value in Vivo's flagship range — Dimensity 9400 and Zeiss for RM 3,499 is hard to argue with. If your budget stops at RM 2,299, the V40 Pro is the pick — especially if you shoot a lot of portraits or selfies. The Zeiss camera tuning is consistent across the range and noticeably better than competitors at equivalent prices.

Best Under RM 2,000

Vivo V40

From RM 1,799 (8GB/256GB)
"Premium design, Zeiss cameras, and a class-leading slim profile — excellent value under RM 1,800."

The V40 brings most of what makes the V40 Pro special at a lower price. You still get the Zeiss camera branding, the same slim elegant design, a large 6.78-inch AMOLED display with 120Hz, and a 5,500mAh battery with 80W charging. The main difference is the chipset — Snapdragon 7 Gen 3 on the Pro vs Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 here, which is slightly less powerful but still handles daily tasks and gaming without issues.

For buyers who want the Vivo V series premium look and feel without going over RM 2,000, the V40 is the obvious choice. It's one of the thinnest phones you can buy in Malaysia at this price and the build quality feels significantly more premium than the price suggests.

Top Pick

Vivo Y300 Pro

From RM 1,299 (8GB/256GB)
"5G, AMOLED, and a big battery for under RM 1,300 — one of the best value phones in Malaysia."

The Y300 Pro is one of the most competitive phones in the RM 1,000–1,500 range in Malaysia. You get a 6.67-inch AMOLED display with 120Hz, a 5,000mAh battery, 44W FlashCharge, and 5G connectivity — a combination that few phones match at this price. The Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 chipset handles everyday tasks comfortably, and the 50MP main camera produces clean results in daylight.

Low-light photography is average, and demanding games will need settings turned down. But as a daily driver for streaming, social media, communication, and casual gaming, the Y300 Pro is hard to fault at RM 1,299. The AMOLED screen alone puts it ahead of most LCD competitors at the same price.

Budget Pick

Vivo Y19s

From RM 649 (4GB/128GB)
"A sensible budget pick — big battery and 5G at a price most people can afford."

The Y19s is Vivo's entry-level offering and it keeps things simple — a 6.68-inch LCD display, MediaTek Helio G85 chipset, 50MP main camera, and a 5,000mAh battery. Performance is basic: social media, YouTube, WhatsApp, and light tasks are fine. Demanding apps and games will struggle. The LCD screen is decent for the price but doesn't match the AMOLED displays you'll find on the Y300 Pro.

What makes the Y19s worth considering is the 5G connectivity at RM 649 — that's rare at this price point in Malaysia. If future-proofing your connection matters and budget is tight, the Y19s is a reasonable starting point. For anything beyond basic use, we'd recommend stretching to the Y300 Pro.

Which Vivo should you buy?

Here's the straightforward breakdown for Malaysian buyers in 2026:

  • Budget under RM 700: Vivo Y19s. Basic performance, but 5G at this price is worth noting.
  • Budget under RM 1,300: Vivo Y300 Pro. AMOLED screen, 5G, solid battery — one of the best value phones in Malaysia at this price.
  • Budget under RM 2,000: Vivo V40. Premium slim design, Zeiss cameras, large AMOLED display — feels significantly more expensive than it is.
  • Budget under RM 2,500: Vivo V40 Pro. Better selfie cameras, faster chipset, and Zeiss portrait tuning that stands out from the competition.
  • Budget under RM 4,000: Vivo X200. Dimensity 9400 and Zeiss optics — flagship performance without flagship pricing.
  • Want the very best: Vivo X200 Pro. 200MP periscope telephoto, 6,000mAh battery, Dimensity 9400 — the most capable Vivo ever made.
SpecShelf final verdict

Vivo's 2026 lineup is strong from top to bottom. The Y300 Pro at RM 1,299 is one of the best value phones in Malaysia right now — AMOLED, 5G, and solid battery life for under RM 1,300 is hard to beat. The V40 at RM 1,799 is the pick for buyers who want a premium-looking phone that photographs well. And the X200 at RM 3,499 is a genuine flagship contender that deserves more attention than it gets in Malaysia. Vivo's Zeiss partnership is paying off — the cameras across the range are consistently impressive.
