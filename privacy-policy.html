Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

We'll keep this short and in plain English. SpecShelf is a small independent site — just a team that genuinely loves phones and wants to help Malaysian buyers make smarter decisions. We're not a big corporation, and we're not here to harvest your data.

What information we collect

We don't ask you to create an account. We don't collect your name, email address, or any personal details unless you contact us directly.

Like most websites, we do use a few third-party tools that collect anonymous usage data:

  • Google Analytics — tells us which pages people visit, how long they stay, and where traffic comes from. All anonymous. We use this to understand what content is useful.
  • Google AdSense — serves ads on the site. Google may use cookies to show ads relevant to you based on your browsing history. This is handled entirely by Google under their own privacy policy.

Cookies

We don't set our own cookies. The cookies on SpecShelf come from Google Analytics and Google AdSense. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Advertising

SpecShelf uses Google AdSense to display ads. These ads help keep the site free. Google may personalise ads based on your previous activity across the web. If you'd prefer not to see personalised ads, you can opt out at adssettings.google.com.

Affiliate links

Some links on SpecShelf may be affiliate links — meaning if you click through and buy something, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. We'll always label these clearly. Our reviews and recommendations are never influenced by affiliate relationships.

Third-party links

We link to external sites — retailer pages, manufacturer specs, and so on. Once you leave SpecShelf, their own privacy policies apply. We're not responsible for what other sites do with your data.

Children's privacy

SpecShelf is a general tech information site. We don't knowingly collect any data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change anything significant, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Nothing sneaky.

Contact us

Questions about privacy? Reach us at our Contact page. We'll get back to you.
