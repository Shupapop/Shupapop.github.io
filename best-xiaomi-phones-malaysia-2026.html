The best Xiaomi phones in Malaysia 2026

Best Overall

Xiaomi 15 Ultra

From RM 5,499 (16GB/512GB)
"The camera phone that challenges everything at any price."

The Xiaomi 15 Ultra is a genuine flagship and one of the best camera phones money can buy in Malaysia right now. Built with Leica in a deep hardware collaboration, the quad-camera system — anchored by a massive 1-inch type sensor — produces images that rival dedicated cameras in low light. Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset, 90W wired charging, and a 5,000mAh battery round out the package.

The display is a 6.73-inch LTPO AMOLED that adapts from 1Hz to 120Hz for efficiency without sacrificing smoothness. It's heavy and premium-feeling, designed for buyers who treat the camera as the main reason to buy a phone. If photography matters to you and you can stretch the budget, the 15 Ultra has very few rivals.

Runner-Up

Xiaomi 14T Pro

From RM 3,299 (12GB/512GB)
"Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 and Leica cameras for under RM 3,500 — outstanding value."

The 14T Pro is arguably the best value flagship in Malaysia right now. Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset — the same chip in phones costing RM 5,000+ — paired with a Leica-tuned triple camera system and 144Hz AMOLED display. At RM 3,299 it's a steal compared to what Samsung and Apple charge for equivalent performance.

Charging is a highlight: 120W HyperCharge fills the 5,000mAh battery from zero to full in about 19 minutes. That is genuinely impressive. The phone also runs HyperOS which is cleaner and faster than older MIUI, with 4 years of OS updates promised. If you want near-flagship performance without the near-flagship price, this is the pick.

Top Pick

Xiaomi 14T

From RM 2,299 (12GB/256GB)
"Leica cameras and a flagship display for mid-range money."

The Xiaomi 14T runs a Dimensity 8300 Ultra chipset — a strong mid-range chip that handles gaming, multitasking and daily use comfortably. You also get the Leica camera branding and tuning, a 144Hz AMOLED display, and 67W fast charging. At RM 2,299 this is excellent bang for ringgit.

The difference vs the 14T Pro is the chipset (Dimensity 8300 Ultra vs Snapdragon 8 Gen 3) and charging speed (67W vs 120W). For daily use those differences won't matter to most people — but if you game hard or want the fastest possible charging, stretch to the Pro. Otherwise the 14T is the smarter buy for budget-conscious buyers who still want Leica quality.

SpecShelf take — Xiaomi flagship range

The 14T Pro is the standout value pick in the flagship tier — Snapdragon 8 Gen 3, Leica, 120W charging at RM 3,299 is genuinely hard to beat. The 15 Ultra is better but costs RM 2,200 more. The 14T is excellent if RM 2,299 is your ceiling. All three run HyperOS with real software support.

Best Mid-Range

Redmi Note 14 Pro+

From RM 1,799 (12GB/512GB)
"The best Redmi Note ever — 200MP camera and 90W charging under RM 1,800."

The Redmi Note 14 Pro+ pushes the mid-range ceiling upward. A 200MP main camera at this price is remarkable — it captures serious detail in good light, and Xiaomi's processing keeps colours natural. The 6.67-inch AMOLED with 120Hz is sharp and smooth, and the 5,110mAh battery with 90W charging is among the best in this price bracket.

It runs Dimensity 1400 Ultra — more than capable for social media, streaming, and casual gaming. PUBG Mobile and Mobile Legends run well at high settings. For Malaysian buyers who want a feature-packed mid-ranger without crossing RM 2,000, the Note 14 Pro+ is the pick of the bunch.

Top Pick

Redmi Note 14 Pro

From RM 1,299 (8GB/256GB)
"50MP AMOLED value that punches well above RM 1,299."

If the Note 14 Pro+ is too much, the standard Redmi Note 14 Pro still delivers a strong package. Same 6.67-inch AMOLED display and 120Hz, same solid build quality, 50MP main camera, and 5,500mAh battery — one of the biggest in this price range. Charging is 45W which is slower than the Pro+ but still reasonable.

The chipset steps down to Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 which is competent for everyday use and light gaming. For students, budget-conscious buyers, or anyone who wants a reliable AMOLED phone with a big battery under RM 1,300, this is the one to get.

Budget Pick

Redmi 13C

From RM 549 (4GB/128GB)
"The most affordable Xiaomi worth recommending in Malaysia."

Under RM 600 for a phone with a 50MP camera, 5,000mAh battery, and a 6.74-inch display — that's the Redmi 13C pitch, and it's a reasonable one. The MediaTek Helio G85 chipset is entry-level, so don't expect fast performance or great gaming. WhatsApp, TikTok, YouTube and basic tasks are handled fine.

The 50MP camera is decent in daylight but struggles in low light. The display is LCD not AMOLED — it's bright enough outdoors but colours aren't as vivid as pricier Redmi models. As a first smartphone, a backup device, or a phone for a family member who needs something simple and affordable, the Redmi 13C does the job at a price very few can argue with.

Which Xiaomi should you buy?

Here's the honest breakdown for Malaysian buyers in 2026:

  • Budget under RM 600: Redmi 13C. Simple, affordable, big battery. Good enough for basic daily use.
  • Budget under RM 1,500: Redmi Note 14 Pro. AMOLED screen, 5,500mAh battery, 5G, solid camera. Hard to beat at RM 1,299.
  • Budget under RM 2,000: Redmi Note 14 Pro+. 200MP camera, 90W charging, AMOLED — better than most phones at this price from any brand.
  • Budget under RM 2,500: Xiaomi 14T. Leica camera system, 144Hz AMOLED, strong Dimensity chip. Genuinely flagship-adjacent.
  • Budget under RM 4,000: Xiaomi 14T Pro. Snapdragon 8 Gen 3, 120W charging, Leica. One of the best value flagships in Malaysia.
  • Want the very best: Xiaomi 15 Ultra. 1-inch sensor, Leica quad-camera, Snapdragon 8 Elite. The camera king.
SpecShelf final verdict

Xiaomi's 2026 lineup is the best value-for-money range in Malaysia right now, full stop. The Redmi Note 14 Pro at RM 1,299 and the Xiaomi 14T Pro at RM 3,299 are both exceptional at their price points. If you want the most phone per ringgit, Xiaomi is almost always the answer — and the 14T Pro specifically embarrasses phones from other brands that cost RM 1,000 to RM 2,000 more.
