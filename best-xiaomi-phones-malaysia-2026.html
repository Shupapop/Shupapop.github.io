What Malaysian buyers ask us most about Xiaomi

We sell phones to Malaysian buyers on Carousell, and many Xiaomi buyers come from Android fans who like to customise their phone. Here are the questions we hear most.

1. Why do Android fans pick Xiaomi?
Android users like freedom: custom widgets, home screen layouts and themes. Xiaomi's HyperOS gives plenty of customisation options. The other big reason is battery. Xiaomi was one of the first brands to use silicon-carbon batteries, which fit much more capacity in the same body, as the 10,000mAh Redmi Note 17 Pro Max shows.

2. How is the gaming performance?
Flagship chips like the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 in the Xiaomi 17 and 17 Ultra are the best choice for heavy games, including big open-world games such as Genshin Impact and Where Winds Meet. Mid-range Redmi Note phones handle PUBG Mobile and Mobile Legends well, but very demanding games need lower graphics. Bigger batteries also mean longer gaming sessions between charges. These are expectations based on the chips and specs, not our own lab tests.

3. Is the Leica camera real?
Yes. Xiaomi's flagship cameras are co-developed with Leica, which covers lens design and colour tuning. This applies to the Xiaomi 17 series, not to Redmi phones. If photography matters most, look at the 17 Ultra.

4. How is the sound?
Flagship Xiaomi phones have stereo speakers that sound good for videos and games. For the best sound, earphones or a Bluetooth speaker make a bigger difference than choosing between models.

5. Why are Xiaomi flagships so expensive now?
This is a common comment. Xiaomi's top phones now compete with Samsung and Apple on price, so they are not the cheap option they used to be. The real value is in the Redmi Note range, where you get huge batteries, AMOLED-class features and strong durability at RM 849 to RM 1,999.

Buying tip from our seller experience

If you game or use your phone heavily, battery size matters more than most people expect. The Redmi Note 17 Pro Max is the best value for battery life, while the Xiaomi 17 Ultra is for photography lovers with a bigger budget.
