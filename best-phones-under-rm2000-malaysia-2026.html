Best Phones Under RM 2,000 in Malaysia (2026)

Published June 2026  ·  By SpecShelf Editorial  ·  5 min read

Not everyone wants to spend RM 5,000 or more on a phone. The truth is, you don't have to. In 2026, the mid-range smartphone market in Malaysia has gotten really good — you can get a phone with a fast processor, a great camera, and all-day battery life without breaking the bank.

This guide is for anyone in Malaysia trying to find a solid phone for under RM 2,000. We looked at what's actually available at local stores and online retailers like Lazada, Shopee, and official brand stores, and picked the ones that give you the most for your money.

💡 Quick tip before you buy: Prices in Malaysia can vary between stores, especially during sale seasons like Shopee 11.11 or Lazada birthday sales. Always check a few places before paying full price. The prices listed here are estimated retail prices as of mid-2026.

1. Redmi Note 15 Pro+ 5G — Best overall value

Redmi Note 15 Pro+ 5G

Xiaomi · 5G · Fast 120W charging

~RM 1,599

If you have a budget of around RM 1,600 and you want the most phone for your money, the Redmi Note 15 Pro+ 5G is hard to beat. Xiaomi's Redmi line has always been popular in Malaysia because it offers features you'd normally find in more expensive phones, but at a much lower price.

The standout feature here is the 120W fast charging. That means you can charge your phone from empty to full in under an hour — much faster than most phones in this price range. For people who are always on the go or forget to charge at night, this is a genuinely useful thing to have.

  • 5G connectivity — future-proof for Malaysia's growing 5G network
  • 120W fast charging — one of the fastest at this price
  • Curved AMOLED display — looks premium despite the price
  • 512GB storage option available — no need to delete photos
Our verdict: Best pick if you want maximum features for minimum spending. Great for students, first-time smartphone upgraders, or anyone who just needs a reliable daily phone without paying flagship prices.

2. Samsung Galaxy A57 — Best for Samsung fans

Samsung Galaxy A57

Samsung · 5G · IP67 water resistant

~RM 2,399

Samsung is still the most trusted phone brand in Malaysia for many people — and for good reason. Their after-sales service centres are easy to find, and most Malaysians already know how to use Samsung's One UI interface. The Galaxy A57 is their 2026 mid-range offering and it comes with some features that are genuinely impressive for the price.

The biggest selling point of the A57 over cheaper alternatives is the IP67 rating — that means it's dust and water resistant. If you've ever dropped your phone in water, or use your phone a lot outdoors, this is worth paying extra for. You also get Samsung's promised software support, which means your phone will keep getting updates for years.

  • IP67 water resistance — survive splashes and light rain
  • Samsung's trusted brand and service network in Malaysia
  • Clean One UI software with long update support
  • 5G ready for future network upgrades
Our verdict: Slightly above RM 2,000 but worth mentioning because Samsung's reliability and water resistance make it worth the stretch for many buyers. Best for people who prefer a familiar, trusted brand with good local support.

What to look for when buying a phone in Malaysia

Before you go and buy, here are a few things that are worth paying attention to — especially if this is your first time buying a mid-range phone.

5G support: Malaysia's 5G network is still expanding, but it's growing fast. If you plan to keep your phone for 3 or more years, getting a 5G-capable phone now means you won't have to upgrade just because your phone can't connect to the newer network. Both phones on this list support 5G.

Storage: 128GB feels like a lot until you have two years of photos and WhatsApp videos on it. If you can afford the higher storage variant, go for it — most phones today don't have a memory card slot, so you can't expand it later.

Where to buy safely in Malaysia: Always buy from official brand stores, authorised resellers, or well-known platforms like Lazada Mall or Shopee Mall (the ones with the official store badge). Avoid buying from random sellers offering prices that seem too good to be true — there are many grey market phones in Malaysia that don't come with local warranty.

Local warranty matters: A phone with Malaysian warranty can be repaired at official service centres here. A grey import might be cheaper, but if something goes wrong you may have to send it overseas or pay full repair costs yourself.

Final thoughts

The under-RM 2,000 segment in Malaysia is genuinely competitive right now. You don't need to spend a lot to get a phone that does everything you need — calls, WhatsApp, TikTok, photos, mobile banking, and more.

Our top pick is the Redmi Note 15 Pro+ 5G for pure value, and the Samsung Galaxy A57 if you want peace of mind from a trusted brand with water resistance and strong local support.
