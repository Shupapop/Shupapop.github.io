The best OPPO phones in Malaysia 2026

Best Overall

OPPO Find X8 Pro

From RM 4,999 (12GB/256GB)
"OPPO's most powerful phone — Hasselblad cameras, Snapdragon 8 Elite, and 100W charging."

The Find X8 Pro is OPPO's flagship and one of the most capable phones you can buy in Malaysia right now. It's built in collaboration with Hasselblad — the legendary camera brand — giving it a quad-camera system that handles portrait, wildlife, landscape and low-light shooting with genuine versatility. The 50MP periscope telephoto alone is worth the price for photography enthusiasts.

Under the hood it runs Snapdragon 8 Elite — the same chipset as Samsung's Galaxy S25 series — with 12GB RAM for smooth multitasking and gaming. The 6.78-inch LTPO AMOLED display is stunning, and 100W SuperVOOC charging fills the 5,910mAh battery in under 45 minutes. This is a phone that genuinely competes with anything at any price.

Runner-Up

OPPO Find X8

From RM 3,699 (12GB/256GB)
"All the Find X8 Pro power in a more compact body, for RM 1,300 less."

The standard Find X8 is the smarter buy for most people. You still get Snapdragon 8 Elite, Hasselblad-tuned cameras, and the same brilliant AMOLED display — just in a slightly smaller 6.59-inch body and without the periscope telephoto of the Pro. The triple camera system (50MP main, 50MP portrait, 50MP ultrawide) is still excellent.

80W SuperVOOC charging is slightly slower than the Pro's 100W but still very fast — 0 to full in about an hour. If you want the flagship OPPO experience without the flagship price tag, this is the one. At RM 3,699 it's excellent value against the competition.

Top Pick

OPPO Reno 13 Pro

From RM 2,499 (12GB/512GB)
"Flagship-style cameras and design at a mid-range price — hard to ignore."

The Reno 13 Pro hits a sweet spot that OPPO has always been good at — premium look, premium camera experience, at a price that doesn't require a flagship budget. The 50MP Sony LYT-600 main sensor produces detailed, accurate images in good and challenging light. The curved 6.83-inch AMOLED display looks premium and the build quality feels expensive.

It runs MediaTek Dimensity 8350 which is a strong upper mid-range chip — gaming performance is solid for PUBG Mobile, Mobile Legends, and Genshin Impact on medium-high settings. 80W SuperVOOC charging and a 5,800mAh battery make this a strong all-day phone. For buyers who want a premium-feeling OPPO without crossing RM 2,500, this is the pick.

SpecShelf take — OPPO flagship & mid-flagship

The Find X8 is the best value in OPPO's flagship range — Snapdragon 8 Elite and Hasselblad cameras for RM 3,699 is genuinely competitive. The Reno 13 Pro at RM 2,499 is the pick if you want premium styling and a great camera without going fully flagship. Both are strong buys for their price points.

Best Under RM 2,000

OPPO Reno 13

From RM 1,799 (8GB/256GB)
"The best all-round OPPO for most Malaysian buyers."

The Reno 13 is where OPPO really earns its reputation in Malaysia. At RM 1,799 you get a 50MP main camera, a 6.59-inch AMOLED display with 120Hz, a clean ColorOS experience, and 45W SuperVOOC charging that's faster than most phones at this price. The Dimensity 8250 chipset handles daily tasks and gaming comfortably.

Battery life is solid at 5,600mAh, and the slim, premium-looking design means it doesn't feel like a budget compromise. For students, young professionals, and anyone who wants a reliable daily driver that looks good and takes decent photos, the Reno 13 is the most balanced OPPO pick in 2026.

Top Pick

OPPO A5 Pro

From RM 1,299 (8GB/256GB)
"Rugged, big battery, 5G — the practical choice under RM 1,300."

The OPPO A5 Pro takes a different approach to the typical mid-range phone. It's built tough — IP69 rating for water and dust resistance, which is rare at this price — and pairs it with a massive 6,000mAh battery that can genuinely last two days on a charge. If durability and battery life matter more than camera quality, this is the OPPO to get.

The 50MP camera is capable in daylight, average in low light. The Snapdragon 6s Gen 3 chipset handles daily tasks well but isn't designed for demanding gaming. For outdoor workers, travellers, or anyone who needs a phone that can take a knock and keep going, the A5 Pro delivers real value at RM 1,299.

Budget Pick

OPPO A38

From RM 699 (4GB/128GB)
"The most affordable OPPO worth buying in Malaysia right now."

Under RM 700 for an OPPO with a 6.56-inch display, 50MP camera, and 5,000mAh battery — that's the A38's pitch, and it's a reasonable one. The Helio G85 chipset is entry-level, so performance is basic. WhatsApp, TikTok, YouTube, and light social media use are handled fine. Don't expect smooth gaming or great low-light photos.

The 33W SUPERVOOC charging is a highlight — unusually fast for a phone at this price point. The build feels solid enough and OPPO's ColorOS is clean and easy to use. As a backup phone, a first smartphone for a family member, or a secondary device, the A38 punches slightly above its weight at RM 699.

Which OPPO should you buy?

Here's the honest breakdown for Malaysian buyers in 2026:

  • Budget under RM 700: OPPO A38. Basic but decent. Good for light daily use and faster charging than you'd expect.
  • Budget under RM 1,300: OPPO A5 Pro. Tough build, massive battery, 5G. Best for outdoor use or if durability matters.
  • Budget under RM 2,000: OPPO Reno 13. The best all-round OPPO for most people — great display, solid camera, clean software.
  • Budget under RM 2,500: OPPO Reno 13 Pro. Premium design, better camera sensor, bigger battery. Worth the extra if you can stretch.
  • Budget under RM 4,000: OPPO Find X8. Snapdragon 8 Elite and Hasselblad cameras for RM 3,699 — outstanding value in the flagship tier.
  • Want the very best: OPPO Find X8 Pro. Periscope telephoto, 100W charging, the most versatile camera setup OPPO has ever made.
SpecShelf final verdict

OPPO's 2026 lineup is one of the strongest the brand has ever put out in Malaysia. The Reno 13 at RM 1,799 is the sweet spot for most buyers — premium feel, solid camera, good battery, reasonable price. The Find X8 at RM 3,699 is the flagship pick if you want to go all-in without paying Samsung Ultra money. And the A5 Pro at RM 1,299 is a genuinely smart buy for anyone who prioritises toughness and battery life over everything else.
