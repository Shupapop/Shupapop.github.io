The best OPPO phones in Malaysia 2026

Best Camera

OPPO Find X9 Pro

RM 5,099 (16GB + 512GB) · promo price of RM 4,999 during the launch month
"OPPO’s Hasselblad flagship, with a huge battery and the best zoom of any phone in this guide."

The Find X9 Pro is OPPO’s top phone, launched in Malaysia in November 2025. It uses the MediaTek Dimensity 9500 chip with 16GB RAM and 512GB storage, a 6.78-inch LTPO AMOLED display at 120Hz, a 50MP autofocus selfie camera, and a 7,500mAh silicon-carbon battery with 80W wired and 50W wireless charging.

Its main strength is the camera system, tuned with Hasselblad and built around a 200MP periscope telephoto lens. OPPO also sells a Hasselblad Teleconverter accessory (RM 999 at launch) that gives a 3.28x optical boost and 230mm reach. In a casual hands-on, our seller zoomed on subjects about 10 metres away and found the photos stayed sharp and clean. That was not a lab test, but the zoom quality impressed us.

OPPO Find X9

RM 3,699 (16GB + 512GB)
"The same chip as the Pro for RM 1,300 less."

The standard Find X9 has the same Dimensity 9500 chip, a 6.59-inch 120Hz AMOLED display and a 200MP main camera with OIS. It has a 7,025mAh battery and a 50MP 3x telephoto. What you give up compared with the Pro is the 200MP periscope telephoto, the adaptive LTPO display and a slightly smaller battery.

Choose it if you want the Find X9 experience and strong performance, but do not need the very best zoom.

What Malaysian buyers ask us most about OPPO

We sell phones to Malaysian buyers on Carousell, so we hear the same questions about OPPO again and again.

1. Can OPPO phones play heavy games?
Yes. Customers who ask about gaming usually find that flagship OPPOs, like the Find X9 series with the Dimensity 9500, run heavy games like Genshin Impact and PUBG Mobile at high settings, much like other flagships. Mid-range Reno models handle most games well at medium to high settings. This is based on chip and spec expectations, not our own lab tests.

2. How fast and easy is the software?
Buyers often tell us ColorOS is friendly and easy to use, with a smooth interface that suits both new and long-time Android users. The Find X9 series runs ColorOS 16.

3. Is OPPO really good for camera lovers?
For the price, yes. The Find X9 Pro has Hasselblad tuning, a 200MP periscope telephoto lens and an optional teleconverter, and in our hands-on the zoom stayed sharp at around 10 metres. If photography is your main reason to buy, the Find X9 Pro is the OPPO to look at.

4. How long does the battery last?
OPPO now uses large silicon-carbon batteries. The Find X9 Pro has 7,500mAh, the Reno 15 has 7,000mAh and the Reno 15 Pro has 6,200mAh, so all of them should last a full day of heavy use.

Buying tip from our seller experience

If camera quality is your top priority, the Find X9 Pro is the OPPO to get, but at RM 5,099 you should also compare it with the Find X9 at RM 3,699. For most people who want a good all-round phone, the Reno 15 at RM 1,999 is the smarter value.

OPPO is the best choice in Malaysia right now for buyers who care about phone cameras. The Find X9 Pro is the star, the Find X9 is the better value, and the Reno 15 is the best mid-range pick. Always compare prices at the official OPPO store with authorised sellers before you buy.
