🎮 What to Look for in a Gaming Phone in Malaysia

🖥️ Display refresh rate: Minimum 120Hz for smooth gameplay. 144Hz and 165Hz give you a competitive edge in shooters like PUBG Mobile.

❄️ Thermal management: Critical in Malaysia's hot climate. Look for vapour chamber cooling or active cooling systems to prevent throttling.

⚡ Chipset: Snapdragon 8 Elite or Dimensity 9400 for top performance. Dimensity 8300 Ultra is the sweet spot for mid-range gaming.

🔋 Battery size: At least 5,000 mAh for extended sessions. Fast charging (67W+) is essential so you can top up quickly between matches.

💾 RAM: Minimum 12GB for gaming in 2026. 16GB keeps games loaded in background without reloading.
