Best Tablets Under RM 3,000 in Malaysia (2026)

Published July 2026  ·  By SpecShelf Editorial  ·  6 min read

Tablets have become one of the most useful devices you can own in Malaysia right now. Whether you're a student attending online classes, a working adult who needs a second screen for Zoom calls, or someone who just wants a bigger screen to watch movies and scroll through social media — a good tablet makes all of that so much more enjoyable.

The good news is that in 2026, you don't need to spend RM 5,000 on an iPad Pro to get a genuinely great tablet experience. There are excellent options available in Malaysia for under RM 3,000 that offer big beautiful screens, fast processors, and long battery life. Here are our top picks.

💡 Tablet or laptop — which should you buy? If you mainly need to type long documents or run heavy software like video editing programs, a laptop is still the better choice. But if your main needs are watching content, reading, drawing, video calls, and light productivity — a tablet is lighter, more portable, and often cheaper for what you actually need.

1. Xiaomi Pad 8 Pro — Best overall tablet under RM 3,000

Xiaomi Pad 8 Pro

Xiaomi · 13.0" LCD · 144Hz · 12,000 mAh · 67W charging

~RM 2,199

The Xiaomi Pad 8 Pro is the tablet we'd recommend to most Malaysian buyers in 2026. It gives you an enormous 13-inch screen, a flagship-level processor, and a massive 12,000 mAh battery — all for around RM 2,199. That's genuinely impressive value for money that's hard to beat at this price point.

The 144Hz display refresh rate means scrolling and animations look silky smooth — much smoother than most laptops and even some more expensive tablets. For watching Malaysian dramas on Netflix, YouTube videos, or reading documents, this screen is simply a pleasure to use every day.

Battery life is one of the Xiaomi Pad 8 Pro's biggest strengths. The 12,000 mAh battery can last a full day of heavy use — streaming, browsing, video calls — and when you do need to charge, the 67W fast charging gets you back to full in under an hour. For students or workers who move around a lot, this is a huge practical advantage.

  • Massive 13" screen — great for multitasking with split-screen apps
  • 144Hz refresh rate — smooth scrolling and animations
  • 12,000 mAh battery — easily lasts a full day
  • 67W fast charging — full charge in under an hour
  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 4 chipset — handles everything smoothly
  • Leica-style camera system for a tablet
Our verdict: The best all-round tablet you can buy in Malaysia for under RM 2,500. Excellent for students, home users, and anyone who wants a big-screen experience for entertainment and light work. The huge battery alone makes it worth considering over the competition.

2. Honor Pad 20 Pro — Best premium tablet under RM 2,000

Honor Pad 20 Pro

Honor · 12.3" OLED · 144Hz · 10,200 mAh · 66W charging

~RM 1,899

If you want the best screen quality in a tablet under RM 2,000, the Honor Pad 20 Pro is the one to get. Unlike most tablets in this price range which use LCD screens, the Honor Pad 20 Pro features a proper OLED display — the same type of screen technology used in premium smartphones. This means richer colours, deeper blacks, and better contrast that makes everything look more vivid and lifelike.

At RM 1,899, the Honor Pad 20 Pro is also the most affordable option on this list, which makes it a great choice for students or anyone on a tighter budget who still wants a quality experience. Honor has been growing its reputation in Malaysia as a brand that offers premium features at accessible prices, and this tablet is a good example of that.

The tablet is impressively thin at just 5.9mm — thinner than most tablets you'll find in this price range — which makes it easy to carry around in a bag or hold for long reading sessions without your arms getting tired.

  • OLED display — richer colours than most tablets in this price range
  • Only 5.9mm thin — one of the slimmest tablets available in Malaysia
  • 144Hz refresh rate — smooth display performance
  • 10,200 mAh battery with 66W fast charging
  • Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 chipset — fast and efficient
  • MagicOS 10 based on Android 16 — clean and easy to use
Our verdict: Best choice if screen quality is your top priority and you want to stay under RM 2,000. The OLED display at this price point is rare and genuinely makes a difference for watching content. Also a great pick for students who need something lightweight to carry to class every day.

Which tablet should you choose?

Both tablets are excellent choices — the right one depends on what matters most to you:

Choose Xiaomi Pad 8 Pro if... You want the biggest screen, longest battery, and best all-round performance for the price
Choose Honor Pad 20 Pro if... You want the best screen quality (OLED), a thinner/lighter design, and want to spend less
For students Either works well — the Honor is lighter to carry, the Xiaomi has more screen space for notes
For entertainment Honor for better picture quality, Xiaomi for bigger screen size

Things to know before buying a tablet in Malaysia

Do you need cellular (SIM card) support? Both tablets on this list are Wi-Fi only models. If you need to use your tablet with a SIM card for mobile data when you're away from home Wi-Fi, look for the LTE or 5G variant — these usually cost a bit more but give you the freedom to use the tablet anywhere.

Keyboard and stylus accessories: If you plan to use your tablet for work or study, check whether the brand sells an official keyboard cover and stylus pen for your model. Both Xiaomi and Honor sell accessories for their tablets, though these are sold separately and add to the total cost.

Where to buy safely in Malaysia: Always buy from official brand stores, Lazada Mall, Shopee Mall, or authorised retailers. For tablets especially, make sure you're getting the Malaysian set with local warranty — grey imports from China may have different software and no local warranty support.

Storage matters more on tablets: Tablets are often used to store movies, photos, and large apps. If possible, go for 256GB or above. Neither of these tablets has a microSD card slot, so what you buy is what you get.

Final thoughts

The tablet market in Malaysia in 2026 offers genuinely great value at the under-RM 3,000 price point. You don't need to spend on an iPad to get a tablet that handles everything most people need day to day.

Our top overall pick is the Xiaomi Pad 8 Pro for its massive screen and incredible battery life, while the Honor Pad 20 Pro wins for those who want an OLED screen and a slimmer, lighter design at a lower price.
