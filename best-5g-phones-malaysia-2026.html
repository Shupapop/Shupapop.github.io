📡 Is 5G Worth It in Malaysia in 2026?

Yes — if you're in KL, Selangor, Penang, or JB. 5G coverage in these areas is solid in 2026 and getting faster. Streaming, downloading, and browsing is noticeably faster on 5G.

Maybe — if you're in other states. 5G rollout outside major cities is still ongoing. Check your carrier's coverage map before deciding.

Future-proofing matters. Even if 5G coverage isn't great in your area now, it will be within 1-2 years. Buying a 5G phone today means your phone stays relevant longer.

5G uses more battery. Keep 5G off when you don't need it — switch to 4G to save battery life during normal daily use.
