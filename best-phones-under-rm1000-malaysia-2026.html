Best Phones Under RM 1,000 in Malaysia (2026)

Published August 2026  ·  By SpecShelf Editorial  ·  5 min read

Not everyone needs — or wants — to spend thousands on a smartphone. In Malaysia, the under-RM 1,000 segment is actually one of the most competitive in the market. Chinese brands like Xiaomi, Realme, and Infinix have been pushing hard to offer more features at lower prices, and in 2026 the results are genuinely impressive.

This guide is for students buying their first phone, parents looking for something simple for an elderly family member, or anyone who just needs a reliable everyday phone without spending a lot. You can absolutely find a good phone for under RM 1,000 in Malaysia — you just need to know what to look for.

💡 What to expect under RM 1,000: At this budget, you'll get a decent camera for WhatsApp and social media, enough storage for apps and photos, good battery life, and a usable screen. What you won't get is the fastest processor or the best low-light camera — those require spending more. But for everyday use, these phones are more than enough.

1. Redmi 14C — Best overall under RM 500

Redmi 14C

Xiaomi · 6.88" display · 5,160 mAh · 50MP camera

~RM 449

The Redmi 14C is one of the best value phones you can buy in Malaysia right now. For under RM 500, you get a massive 6.88-inch screen, a 50MP main camera, and a 5,160 mAh battery that easily lasts two days on a single charge. Xiaomi has packed in features that would have cost twice as much just a few years ago.

This phone is especially popular with students and older users who want a simple, reliable daily phone. The large screen makes it easy to read text, watch videos, and use WhatsApp comfortably. The battery life means you don't have to worry about charging it every night — it will easily get through a full day and more.

  • Huge 6.88" screen — great for older users and media watching
  • 50MP main camera — takes decent photos for social media
  • 5,160 mAh battery — easily lasts 2 days for light users
  • Available at Xiaomi Malaysia official stores and major platforms
  • Runs HyperOS based on Android — clean and easy to use
Our verdict: The best budget phone in Malaysia for under RM 500. Perfect for students, seniors, or anyone who needs a simple reliable daily phone without spending much. Don't expect flagship performance — but for calls, WhatsApp, TikTok and photos, it handles everything just fine.

2. Realme C75 — Best battery life under RM 700

Realme C75

Realme · 6.72" display · 6,000 mAh · IP54 splash resistant

~RM 649

If battery life is your top priority and you have a slightly bigger budget, the Realme C75 is worth a look. It packs a 6,000 mAh battery — one of the largest you'll find at this price point in Malaysia — and it also comes with IP54 splash resistance, which means it can survive light rain and accidental water splashes. That's a feature you rarely see under RM 700.

Realme has been growing its presence in Malaysia steadily, and their service centres are available in major cities. The C75 runs a clean version of Android that's easy to navigate even for people who aren't very tech-savvy.

  • 6,000 mAh battery — outstanding battery life at this price
  • IP54 splash resistant — survives rain and water splashes
  • 6.72" display — comfortable size for daily use
  • Clean Android interface — easy for beginners
  • Available at Realme Malaysia stores and Shopee/Lazada Mall
Our verdict: Best choice if you want maximum battery life and some water resistance under RM 700. Great for people who work outdoors, travel frequently, or just hate having to charge their phone every day.

Samsung Galaxy A16

Samsung · 6.7" display · 5,000 mAh · 6 years of OS updates

~RM 899

If you want the Samsung brand name and ecosystem but don't want to spend over RM 1,000, the Galaxy A16 is your best option in Malaysia. Samsung has made a bold promise with this phone — six years of OS updates. That means if you buy it today, Samsung will keep updating the software until 2031. For a phone that costs under RM 1,000, that's an extraordinary commitment.

Samsung's after-sales service is also one of the strongest in Malaysia. You can find Samsung service centres in almost every major mall, which matters a lot if your phone ever needs repair. For buyers who value brand trust and long-term support over raw performance, the Galaxy A16 makes a strong case.

  • 6 years of OS and security updates — unmatched at this price

Our verdict: Best for buyers who want a trusted brand with the longest software support in this price range. The 6-year update promise makes it one of the smartest long-term buys under RM 1,000 in Malaysia — you won't need to upgrade just because your phone stopped getting updates.

What to watch out for when buying a budget phone in Malaysia

⚠️ Watch out for grey imports: Budget phones are the most common category for grey market imports in Malaysia. Always buy from official brand stores or Lazada Mall / Shopee Mall (with the official store badge). A grey import might be RM 50–100 cheaper but comes with no local warranty — if anything goes wrong, you're on your own.

Storage and RAM: Budget phones often come in multiple variants — for example, 4GB RAM + 128GB storage, or 6GB RAM + 128GB storage. The extra RAM makes a noticeable difference for running multiple apps at the same time. If the price difference is small, go for the higher RAM variant.

5G vs 4G at this budget: Most phones under RM 1,000 in Malaysia are 4G. 5G phones in this price range do exist but they're rare and often sacrifice other features to hit the price point. For most users in Malaysia, 4G is still perfectly fast for everything — streaming, WhatsApp, mobile banking. Don't pay extra for 5G unless you specifically need it.

Camera reality check: Marketing numbers like "50MP camera" sound impressive, but on budget phones the camera hardware and software processing are still limited. Expect decent photos in good lighting — but don't expect great low-light or portrait shots. For those you'll need to spend at least RM 1,500+.

Final thoughts

The under-RM 1,000 phone market in Malaysia is surprisingly good in 2026. You can get a phone that handles all your daily needs — calls, WhatsApp, social media, photos, mobile banking, and YouTube — without breaking the bank.

Our top pick for pure value is the Redmi 14C under RM 500, the Realme C75 if battery life is your priority, and the Samsung Galaxy A16 if you want a trusted brand with the longest software support available at this price.
